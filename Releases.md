# Release Notes

## 1.3 (in progress)

*Planned work:*

* Extended the GLMO with data from vocational schools (*Berufsschulen*).



## 1.2

* Extended the GLMO with historical occupational data from the former German Democratic Republic (GDR), closing the gap of missing GDR data.
* Extended the GLMO with historical versions of occupational taxonomies.
* Enabled temporal analyses of occupational structures.

**Publication:**  
Esser, A. M.; Dörpinghaus, J.; Reiser, T.; Steiner, P. & Runk, I.  
*GLMO 1.2: Integrating Historical Occupational Data into the German Labor Market Ontology*  
Journal of Web Semantics, 2026



## 1.1

+ Extended the GLMO with online data.

**Publication:**  
Martić, D.; Fischer, A. & Dörpinghaus, J.  
*Extending the German Labor Market Ontology with Online Data*   
INFORMATIK, Gesellschaft für Informatik e.V., 2024, 2019-2030



## 1.0

* Introduced the German Labor Market Ontology (GLMO) as a top-level ontology and central knowledge base for German labor market data, integrating various taxonomies.

**Publication:**  
Dörpinghaus, J.; Binnewitt, J.; Winnige, S.; Hein, K. & Krüger, K.  
*Towards a German Labor Market Ontology: Challenges and Applications*  
Applied Ontology, SAGE, 2023, 18, 343-365
